## Research summary (sources in web searches, 2026-08-31)

- AU trades shortage: ~74,000 worker shortfall 2026 (NSC); Infrastructure
  Australia projects 300,000 construction-worker shortage by 2027; vacancy
  fill rates ~54–68%; wages up 8–15% in 2 yrs; ~48% of apprenticeships don't
  complete; 25%+ of AU construction workforce over 55.
- Turnover in construction ~48%/yr (voluntary ~21%); replacement cost
  **50–200% of annual salary**.
- Why good people leave (<10% cite pay as primary): **management/supervisor
  quality** ("people don't leave bad jobs, they leave bad bosses"), no
  visible progression path, feeling undervalued, unstable rosters, admin
  burden. First 90 days decide it. "Panic hiring" is the silent killer — a
  bad hire damages the whole crew's retention.
- Levers that work: supervisor management training ($500–1,500, highest ROI),
  structured onboarding (week-1/month-1 check-ins → +60% first-year
  retention), stay interviews (quarterly, 3 questions), visible career path
  (apprentice → improver → lead installer → contracts manager, with
  timelines), pay benchmarking yearly, keep a **pre-screened candidate pool**
  so openings are choices not emergencies.
- Fake-application landscape: LLM-fabricated resumes, bot sprays, identity
  misrepresentation, AI-coached interviews. Red flags: generic/"perfect"
  resumes, timeline inconsistencies, mass-application phrasing repetition,
  scripted answers, off-channel communication. Defences: 10-min structured
  phone screen, **4–5 layers of follow-up questions** (fakes break at layer
  3), licence/cert verification (White Card, Working at Heights, QBCC —
  public registers), reference checks.

## What was built (all dev-first, 27→28 tests green)

Lean FastAPI + SQLite app, reusing xjobs patterns (scrypt auth, LLM
dispatcher, pymupdf parsing). One page UI (no build step), mobile-first.

- **Applicants**: add with resume (pdf/docx/txt) → auto-parse → one-click LLM
  screening (~6s, ~$0.001 DeepSeek): score 0–100, verdict
  (hire_priority|consider|weak|likely_fake), red flags, consistency issues,
  AI-generation likelihood, licence scan, layered phone-screen questions,
  plain-English notes_for_boss. Pipeline: new → phone_screen → interview →
  trial → hired | rejected | **pool** (anti-panic-hiring list). Verification
  checklist (white_card, working_at_height, qbcc_licence, references,
  police_check, first_aid). Notes + timeline.
- **Staff**: records (role, rate, site, supervisor, skills), onboarding
  milestones (day1/week1/month1/day90), stay interviews (3 questions + risk
  low|medium|high), early-warning flags (absence|late|complaint|engagement|
  other, resolvable), exits (reason picklist). Dashboard aggregates exits by
  reason + at-risk staff.
- **E2E proof**: genuine 14-yr roofer → 85/100 hire_priority, all tickets
  detected, caught the deliberate "Roffing" typo; AI-template resume →
  10/100 likely_fake, AI-likelihood high.
